Radhika Venigalla Founder, Finsavvys
Our story

From dentistry to empowering families

Radhika began her career in dentistry before relocating to the U.S., where her husband practices dentistry in Massachusetts.

She paused her own dental career to raise their two daughters. That life transition — and the financial questions that came with it — inspired Finsavvys: a place where families can learn how money really works, get a plan that fits them, and discover new opportunities to build income.

Outside of work, Radhika loves baking, cooking, painting, decorating her home, volunteering, and traveling with her family.
